Transfer Wallet by Wallet Nation

Send Bitcoin to someone you don't know — safely.

Transfer Wallet is a non-custodial, 2-of-3 multisig Bitcoin transfer with chargeback-style protection. Funds are held on-chain until both people approve. If something goes wrong, an independent reviewer can return the Bitcoin to the sender — but nobody, including us, can send it anywhere else.

How it works

1. Three keys, two needed

Each transfer creates a 2-of-3 multisig address. The Sender, the Receiver and an independent Reviewer each hold one key. Any two can move the funds.

2. Held until both approve

The Sender funds the address. The Bitcoin sits on-chain — not in our account — until Sender and Receiver both sign the release.

3. Protection if it goes wrong

If there's a dispute, the Reviewer can co-sign either release to the Receiver or a return to the Sender. Settlement destinations are locked, so the Reviewer can never redirect funds.

  • Non-custodial: no single party — including Wallet Nation — can move funds alone.
  • Chargeback-style protection for Bitcoin, which normally has no way to reverse a payment.
  • Plain-language flow: invite by email, fund, approve.
